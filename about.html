About WheelGenerator

Making decision-making fun, fast, and completely unbiased.

Welcome to WheelGenerator, your ultimate interactive spin wheel tool designed to make random selection simple, engaging, and fair. Whether you're hosting a giveaway, running a classroom activity, deciding what to eat for dinner, or picking teams for game night, our platform is built to deliver smooth performance with zero hassle.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a clean, accessible, and highly customizable decision-maker that requires no registration or fees. We believe making choices should be exciting, which is why we built a tool packed with rich themes, satisfying sound effects, physics-based rotation algorithms, and instant shareability.

Why Choose WheelGenerator?

  • Truly Random & Fair: Our wheels use physics-based velocity and unbiased angle detection so every entry has an equal chance.
  • Privacy First: Your entries, wheel choices, and spin history remain on your browser via local storage. We don't store your personal custom inputs.
  • Fully Customizable: Choose from vibrant color palettes, customizable options, sound settings, and presets built for every scenario.
  • 100% Free & Responsive: Spin seamlessly on desktops, tablets, or mobile phones whenever you need to make a choice on the fly.
