About WheelGenerator

Delivering high-performance, transparent, and completely random decision tools for users worldwide.

Welcome to WheelGenerator, a web application designed to simplify decision-making through customizable, interactive randomizer utilities. Whether you are an educator selecting students, an event host running a giveaway, a team leader facilitating icebreakers, or an individual deciding daily activities, our platform provides instant, mathematically fair random selection.

Our Mission

Our goal is to offer a reliable, accessible, and user-centric utility platform that operates seamlessly across all modern hardware. WheelGenerator is completely free to use, requires no mandatory user registration, and prioritizes fast loading speeds, responsive UI design, and rigorous client-side data privacy.

Why Choose WheelGenerator?

  • Algorithmic Integrity: Decisions are generated using secure random calculation algorithms to ensure equal probability for every listed entry.
  • Client-First Privacy Architecture: Your custom entry lists and session histories are saved exclusively within your browser's local storage. We do not collect or store your private list data on external servers.
  • Extensive Customization Options: Tailor wheel titles, visual themes, animation speeds, audio cues, and segment proportions to suit any scenario.
  • Cross-Platform Optimization: Fully optimized for desktop displays, tablets, and mobile smartphones for zero-friction access anywhere.

Editorial & Policy Compliance

WheelGenerator is maintained in strict alignment with Webmaster Quality Guidelines and Google AdSense Program Policies. We continuously audit our platform to ensure fast response times, high content standards, full accessibility, and transparent privacy practices.
