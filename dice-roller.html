About This Tool

Free Online Dice Roller — Roll Any Dice

Our free online dice roller is the most complete virtual dice simulator available. Whether you play Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu, or any other tabletop RPG, you'll find every die type you need — D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20, and D100 — all in one place.

Roll multiple dice at once, add modifiers, use the 4D6 drop lowest mechanic for D&D character creation, or define a completely custom die with any number of sides. The mobile-friendly design even supports shake-to-roll on phones and tablets.

All rolls use cryptographically secure randomness via the Web Crypto API — ensuring truly unbiased results for every session. Your roll history is kept throughout your session so you can refer back to past results.
