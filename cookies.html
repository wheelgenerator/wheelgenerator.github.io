Cookies Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how WheelGenerator ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies, tracking technologies, and browser storage mechanisms when you access our interactive website located at https://wheelgenerator.github.io/.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They enable site performance, security features, and preferences tracking. In addition to traditional cookies, we utilize web browser LocalStorage to save your customized wheel options, custom titles, color choices, and preferences locally without transferring data to remote servers.

2. Categories of Cookies and Technologies We Use

  • Essential Browser LocalStorage: Used to maintain your active session state, custom entry lists, sound configurations, and spin history directly inside your browser without requiring personal accounts.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Integrated through service providers like Google Analytics to track aggregated statistical data, user navigation flows, device types, and page loading performance to ensure seamless operational quality.
  • Advertising & Targeting Cookies: Deployed by Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners to deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements, prevent ad fraud, limit repetitive ad exposure, and evaluate ad campaign effectiveness.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookies & Opt-Out

Google operates as a third-party vendor on WheelGenerator. Google uses DART cookies to serve advertisements to site visitors based on their visit to this site and other websites across the Internet. You may opt out of the use of DART cookies or personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads & Privacy Policy Settings page.

4. How to Manage and Control Cookies

You have full control over cookie settings. Most web browsers allow you to block, delete, or manage cookie preferences through their settings menu. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies across network platforms via the Network Advertising Initiative Choice Page or Your Online Choices. Please note that disabling essential browser features may alter certain interface settings.
