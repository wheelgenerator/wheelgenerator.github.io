Cookies Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how WheelGenerator uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website (https://wheelgenerator.github.io/).

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, enhance navigation, and provide web reporting information.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential LocalStorage: Used to save your active wheel entries, selected color theme, sound toggle settings, and spin history within your client browser.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Placed by Google Analytics to help us understand visitor behavior, page views, and traffic sources.
  • Advertising Cookies: Set by Google AdSense to deliver relevant advertisements tailored to user interests and track ad campaign performance.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site and uses DART cookies to serve ads based upon visits to our site and other web pages. You can choose to decline DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.

4. How to Manage and Control Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences directly in your web browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block, refuse, or delete cookies. Note that disabling cookies may affect certain functionality on the site.
