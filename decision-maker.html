About This Tool

Random Decision Maker — Make Choices Effortlessly

Decision fatigue is real. Whether you're choosing what to eat, where to travel, or which job offer to accept, making decisions takes mental energy. Our free decision maker online removes that burden by introducing an element of randomness and fun into the process.

Simply enter your options into the decision picker, hit the big Decide button, and our tool will randomly select one for you. It uses JavaScript's crypto.getRandomValues() API for genuine randomness — not a fake random number that favors certain options.

Perfect as a yes or no decision maker, a random choice maker for group activities, or just to stop overthinking your next meal. Give it a try — your decision is just one click away.
