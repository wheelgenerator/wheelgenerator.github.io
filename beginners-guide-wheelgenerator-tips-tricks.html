New to WheelGenerator? Here's everything you need to know to go from a blank wheel to a fully set-up spin in a couple of minutes — plus a few hidden features most people miss at first.

🎡 Open the Free Wheel Generator

1Getting Started: Your First Wheel

Open the wheel app and you'll see a default set of entries already loaded — replace them with your own by editing each one directly, or clear the list and start fresh. There's no sign-up step; everything works immediately in your browser.

2Bulk Import: The Fastest Way to Add Entries

Instead of typing entries one at a time, open the bulk-add option and paste your full list, one item per line. This is the single biggest time-saver for anyone setting up a wheel with more than a handful of entries — class rosters, giveaway entrants, or a long food list all go in within seconds.

3Using Spin History

Every spin gets logged, so you can always check what's already come up without relying on memory. This matters most for raffles and giveaways, where knowing exactly who's already won avoids repeat-winner mistakes.

4Sound Effects and When to Turn Them Off

The spin comes with a satisfying ticking sound and a win chime by default, which adds energy for live giveaways or classroom use. If you're spinning somewhere quieter — a shared office, a work brainstorm — the sound toggle in the toolbar mutes everything instantly.

5Sharing Your Wheel with a Link

Once your wheel is set up, use the share option to generate a link that reproduces your exact entries and settings for anyone who opens it. This is useful for handing a wheel off to a co-host, publishing it alongside a giveaway announcement, or reusing the same setup across devices.

6Using WheelGenerator on Mobile

The whole app is fully responsive, so you can build and spin a wheel entirely from a phone — handy for teachers moving around a classroom or hosts running a party without a laptop nearby. Tap the canvas directly, or use the spin button if you prefer.

7A Few Hidden Features Worth Knowing

  • Remove after spin — automatically deletes the winning entry, ideal for raffles and classroom cold-calling
  • Fullscreen mode — expands the wheel for presentations or streaming overlays
  • Theme presets — quickly swap the entire color scheme without editing individual slices; see our themes guide
  • Entry sorting and shuffling — reorganize a long list without retyping it

8Common Beginner Questions

Most first-time questions come down to: how many entries can I add (100+, with automatic text sizing), does it cost anything (no, it's completely free), and do I need an account (no, everything runs locally in your browser).
